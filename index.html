⚠️ High Voltage Safety Warning

Electricity can seriously injure or kill. Always follow safety procedures before starting any electrical work.

✅ Turn OFF power before working
✅ Use insulated tools
✅ Wear proper PPE
✅ Check voltage before touching wires
✅ Ensure proper earthing
✅ Never work with wet hands
