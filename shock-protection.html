⚡ Electrical Shock Protection

What is Electric Shock?

Electric shock occurs when electrical current passes through the human body. It can cause burns, injuries, cardiac arrest and even death.

Common Causes of Electric Shock

  • Touching live wires
  • Damaged insulation
  • Wet working conditions
  • Poor earthing
  • Faulty electrical equipment

Shock Prevention Rules

  • Switch OFF power before working
  • Use insulated tools
  • Wear insulated gloves
  • Test voltage before touching conductors
  • Use proper earthing
  • Never work with wet hands

First Aid for Electric Shock

  • Turn OFF the power supply
  • Do not touch the victim directly
  • Use a dry wooden object if needed
  • Call emergency services
  • Provide CPR if trained

⚠️ Warning

Always test before touch. Never assume a conductor is de-energized.
