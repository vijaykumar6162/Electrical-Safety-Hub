🦺 PPE Safety Guide

Safety Helmet

Protects the head from falling objects and accidental impacts.

Insulated Gloves

Protects workers from electric shock while handling conductors.

Safety Goggles

Protects eyes from sparks, dust and arc flash particles.

Safety Shoes

Provides insulation and protection from electrical hazards.

Arc Flash Protection

Special PPE used while working on high-energy electrical panels.
